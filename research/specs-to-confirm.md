# Specs to confirm with supplier

None of these numbers are used anywhere in the page copy or schema defaults.
Confirm each with the supplier, then enter the real value into the matching
setting in the Shopify theme editor (Nuvia Product Specs section). Market
range is reference only, pulled from `research/product-brief.md` §2 —
Nuvia's actual spec may fall outside it.

- [ ] **Node count** — market range: 4–16 nodes (peanut/yoke-style units typically 4–8)
- [ ] **Node type/material** — market range: hard plastic vs. soft-touch/silicone tips (varies by brand)
- [ ] **Max heat temperature** — market range: ~104–113°F (40–45°C)
- [ ] **Number of heat levels** — market range: most listings offer 1–3 heat levels
- [ ] **Number of intensity/speed levels** — market range: 3 is the modal spec across comparable listings
- [ ] **Number of massage modes** (e.g. kneading only vs. kneading + pulsating + oscillating) — market range: 1–3 modes
- [ ] **Battery capacity (mAh)** — market range: 1800–2500 mAh
- [ ] **Runtime per charge, heat OFF** — market range: 70 min–3 hrs (varies widely by brand)
- [ ] **Runtime per charge, heat ON** — typically shorter than heat-off runtime; not consistently reported in comparable listings, get this directly from supplier
- [ ] **Charge time (empty to full)** — market range: ~2–5 hrs
- [ ] **Charging port type** — market range: USB-C is most common in newer listings; confirm cable included
- [ ] **Auto shut-off timer** — market range: 15 min is near-universal in this category
- [ ] **Unit weight** — market range: ~1.3–2.3 lbs (0.6–1.0 kg)
- [ ] **Outer fabric material** — confirm exact material name (e.g. knit polyester, velour) and washability (hand-wash cover only vs. fully washable)
- [ ] **Noise level (dB) if available** — not consistently published in comparable listings; nice-to-have, not required for launch copy
- [ ] **Strap max extension / neck-size fit range** — flagged in negative reviews as a fit-complaint driver; confirm if there's a stated size limit
- [ ] **What's included in the box** — confirm exact contents and cable type before finalizing the "What's in the Box" accordion copy
- [ ] **Certifications** (FCC, CE, RoHS, etc.) if you want to reference them — not currently used in copy
- [ ] **Warranty duration**, if different from the 90-day money-back guarantee (confirmed separately as a business decision, not a supplier spec)
